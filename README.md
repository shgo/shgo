### 🔬 About me

I'm a machine learning researcher working at the intersection of **probabilistic generative modeling**, **structured learning**, and **natural language processing**. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from **UNICAMP**. Today, at the School of Applied Mathematics (EMAp) of Fundação Getulio Vargas, I study **Generative Flow Networks (GFlowNets)**: how to scale them, distribute them, and make them practical. I also work on **NLP for Brazilian Portuguese**, especially information extraction from clinical text.

### 🧭 Research interests

- 🌊 **GFlowNets & amortized inference**: hierarchical, cooperative, and parallel training of generative flow networks
- 🧩 **Structured sparsity & multi-task learning**: group-sparse models that learn how tasks relate to each other
- 🗣️ **Natural Language Processing**: information and event extraction, LLMs for low-resource and clinical Portuguese
